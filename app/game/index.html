    Marswalk 1 Field Simulator

    Walk a real route on Jezero Crater terrain measured by NASA's HiRISE camera. Document four outcrops Perseverance sampled, inspect the Three Forks sample depot, and get home before your suit runs out.

    Speed ×N moves you and the mission clock N times faster together, so oxygen used per metre walked stays at NASA's measured rates.
