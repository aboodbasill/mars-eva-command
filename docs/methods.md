# Methods

1. **Acquisition** (`terrain/01–02`). The CTX DTM, CTX ortho and rover JSON are downloaded in full. The 1.8 GB HiRISE DTM and the 7.4 GB HiRISE ortho are uncompressed stripped GeoTIFFs. Only the AOI window is read, using parallel HTTP range requests with strip offsets parsed by tifffile. The DTM is read in full within the window; the ortho uses every 4th 25 cm row with a 4-column mean, plus full 25 cm chips (160 m square) at each site. The SIM 3464 geodatabase and THEMIS rasters are extracted from the remote zip by range reads.
2. **AOI.** The bounding box of the lander, stations and depot in local metres, plus 1 km, snapped to the 20 m CTX lattice: 4,920 × 2,860 m.
3. **Reprojection** (`terrain/03`). Everything goes onto shared local eqc grids (1, 5, 6 and 20 m) with a common origin. Elevation is warped bilinearly, then re-masked wherever the warped validity mask is below 1, so voids never blend.
4. **Terrain** (`terrain/04`). Horn slope on true metres, NaN unless all 9 cells are valid. Each 5 m cell takes the maximum 1 m slope, and provenance is HiRISE (≥ 90 % valid), CTX-only, or none.
5. **Routing** (`routing/engine.py`). 8-neighbour graph with no corner cutting. Symmetric cost d·(1+(s/15)²)·p with blocked cells excluded. A* uses a straight-line heuristic (admissible because every factor is ≥ 1). It is cross-checked against an independent Dijkstra and SciPy on random grids.
6. **Return field.** Dijkstra from the lander, which on a symmetric graph equals cost-to-lander. It also tracks the physical length of the least-cost path. AOI-boundary truncation is flagged.
7. **Environment** (`environment/01`). PDS4 labels are parsed first and units asserted. Nominal-mode pressure (barocap 1) and mast ATS 1–3 air temperature are aggregated into 30-minute LMST means with valid counts; empty bins stay null. Ls is computed from UTC.
8. **Recon** (`recon/recon.py`). 100 m blocks. Target: mean HiRISE 5 m slope. Predictors use CTX and THEMIS only. Validation uses 500 m vertical strips (2 test, 1 calibration, the rest train) with 100 m buffers. Baselines are the training mean, raw CTX slope, and CTX slope with a linear correction. The model is gradient boosting with fixed hyperparameters, with a split-conformal 80 % interval. The release gate needs ≥ 10 % MAE gain and ≥ 0.70 coverage.
9. **Validation and export** (`08`, `09`). See `docs/validation.md`. The build ID is a content hash, so a new build invalidates the offline cache.
